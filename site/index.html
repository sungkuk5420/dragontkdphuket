About the gym

We don't just teach. We build champions.

Dragon Gym Taekwondo Phuket has been teaching on the island for more than ten years under Korean head coach Yong Taek Yoo and a team of professional instructors. We are a Ministry of Education licensed private school.

Our alumni include the 2022 SEA Games gold medallist Thanakrit "Hammann" Yodrak (men's −58 kg), youth world and Asian medallists, and several Thai national-team members. What we are proudest of, though, is every child who grows up with confidence, discipline and respect.

Our main gym is in Suan Luang in the heart of Phuket Town, with two more branches at Boat Lagoon (Ko Kaeo) and Srisoonthorn (Thalang) so families across the island can train close to home.

      FAQ

      Questions parents ask

      What age can my child start?

      Kids Sport classes start from 2–3 years old. Basic taekwondo classes start from about 5 years old, with no upper age limit.

      Does my child need experience?

      No. Basic classes start from zero and belt tests are held regularly.

      How much does it cost?

      Fees are monthly with unlimited classes at your branch. Please ask for current prices on LINE or by phone.

      Is the trial class really free?

      Yes. The first trial is free — just send the child's name, age and preferred branch in advance.

      Do we need to buy a uniform right away?

      No. Wear sportswear for the trial. After enrolling you can buy a dobok from the gym.
